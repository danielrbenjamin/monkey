How it works

Record once.
Share forever.

No setup, no configuration, no learning curve. If you can click a button, you can make a guide.

1 — Hit record

Click the Monkey icon in your toolbar and press Start Recording. A small badge appears on the page so you always know you're live.

2 — Do your thing

Navigate, click, fill out forms — work exactly as you normally would. Monkey silently captures a screenshot and generates a description for every interaction.

3 — Share your guide

Stop recording and open your guide. Edit descriptions, reorder steps, then export as a self-contained HTML file, copy as Markdown, or print to PDF.

Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Monkey is lean by design. No cloud sync, no paywalls, no accounts — just clean, accurate guides generated entirely in your browser.

Auto screenshots

Every click triggers an automatic screenshot. No manual camera button, no missed moments.

Smart annotations

A red rectangle is drawn around the exact element you interacted with — not just a vague circle in the middle of the screen.

Auto-generated descriptions

Monkey reads ARIA labels, button text, and placeholder attributes to write step descriptions for you. Edit anything with a single click.

Drag to reorder

Grab any step and drag it to where it belongs. Or use the up/down arrows. Delete steps you don't need.

Three export formats

Export as a self-contained HTML file (with screenshots embedded), copy as Markdown, or use your browser's print dialog to save as PDF.

100% private

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your screenshots, your workflow, your data — none of it ever touches a server.
